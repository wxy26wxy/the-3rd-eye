## Strengths
- The wire format matches the zod schema exactly:
  - `CleanupRequest` uses synthesized `Encodable`, so nil keys are left out.
  - `label` is always sent as `"positive"`/`"negative"`, and `box` is 4 Doubles.
  - Points are clamped to [0, w] × [0, h], and a box is only sent when x0 < x1 and y0 < y1.
  - The client never sends negative-only points without ids or a box.
  - The 10-point cap is enforced.
  - `candidateIds` come from server candidates (6 at most), in candidate order.
  - The chosen ids go with every points/box edit, as the final contract says.
- Decoding follows the backend:
  - `imageSize` is optional on the photo answer.
  - `GET /artworks/{id}` takes the presigned top-level `originalUrl`, not the `Artwork.originalUrl` pathname.
  - `details` is read leniently.
  - The cleanup timeout of 300 s is above the server's 270 s deadline.
- Coordinates are converted to `imageSize`, not to the UIImage size. EXIF is handled upstream: `ImageTools.normalized` bakes in the orientation and `jpeg` strips metadata, so the server's EXIF rotation does nothing and the local aspect ratio matches the stored original.
- The auto-skip rule is unchanged (`FindingDrawingsView.swift:31`: at most 1 candidate and `cleanedUrl != nil`). Design ready gets the redo entry point.
- The debug hooks really are Debug-only (checked on the Release binary), and the test photo is generated, not bundled.
- The UI test proves both ids reach the mock, and that a centre tap arrives as about (600, 800) in image pixels.
- The handoff, progress entry and ADR are written. All strings are in the catalog.

### Critical

### Important

1. **Add area, Remove area and Draw a box cannot be used with VoiceOver, and there is no non-touch alternative.**
   - **Where:** `ios/App/Screens/Create/AdjustCutoutView.swift:214-236`, `:200-202`, `:353-382`.
   - **What:** the only way to add a mark or draw a box is a tap or drag on an inaccessible `Color.clear` layer. The photo container is `.contain`, so VoiceOver cannot activate the photo itself. The mode picker and the instruction text are still exposed to VoiceOver, so a VoiceOver user can choose "Draw a box" and then has nothing they can do with it.
   - **Why:** the brief requires these modes to be reachable with VoiceOver, or at least through an explicit non-touch alternative. This app is built for accessibility first.
   - **Fix:** add `accessibilityAction(named:)` custom actions to each piece (thumbnail and chip): "Box around this piece" (sets `box` to its `bbox`) and "Add the area around this piece". Alternatively, give the photo element custom actions (for example "Add area at the centre", or per-piece points at the bbox centre). Hide the mode picker and instruction from VoiceOver, or have them explain the custom actions. Add a unit test for the action-to-request mapping.

2. **VoiceOver reads each piece twice.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:281` (chip) and `:346` (thumbnail), both using `CandidateToggleSemantics` with the label "Piece n".
   - **Why:** the brief explicitly asks for no duplicate reading. Swipe navigation becomes Piece 1, Piece 2, …, Piece 1, Piece 2, and the UI tests need `firstMatch` to work around it.
   - **Fix:** hide the on-photo chips with `.accessibilityHidden(true)` and keep the thumbnail strip as the list. Alternatively, merge the two into one element. Then drop `firstMatch` from `AdjustCutoutUITests` so a duplicate makes the tests fail.

3. **The "Remove area" instruction promises something the backend does not do.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:30`, with the logic in `CutoutGeometry.swift:63-68`.
   - **What:** the screen says "Tap the photo where the cutout should leave something out." Under the contract (api.md T080 §2), a negative point removes the whole connected piece it lands on, and carving part of a piece away is not supported. Take the auto-selected one-piece redo, which is the common case: a "Remove area" tap on the piece deletes all of it, and the server answers `422 no_drawing_found`.
   - **Why:** the creator follows the instruction and gets an error, or loses a piece they wanted to keep.
   - **Fix:**
     - Reword the instruction to "Tap a piece to leave it out of the cutout. To trim one piece, draw a box around what should stay."
     - On the client, warn (or refuse) when a negative mark lands inside the bbox of the only selected piece and no box is drawn.
     - Map a 422 from cleanup to that same advice.

4. **"Preview cutout" saves the cut on the server, but the app treats it as a throwaway preview.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:437-453`, `:478-486`, `:107`.
   - **What:** the backend's `cleanup` writes the new `cleanedUrl`, mask and alt text to the artwork. It also rebuilds every product made from it, and can move a published product back to draft. After Preview, "Back to photo" followed by Retake or the system Back button leaves the server artwork changed. `app.flow.artwork` (shown on Design ready, and the source for listing) still has the old cut. In redo mode after a product exists (Design ready → Yes, list it → Back → Adjust cutout), a mere "preview" rebuilds and possibly unpublishes products.
   - **Fix:** two options.
     - Treat Preview as a commit: on success set `app.flow.artwork = result.artwork` and `cutoutNotice`, and say in the button hint that it saves the cutout.
     - Or, in redo mode, skip Preview and only cut on "Use this".
   - Either way, document the behaviour in the handoff.

5. **In-flight requests outlive the screen, and later navigate the wrong path.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:103-116`, `:132`, `:478-486`.
   - **What:** the unstructured `Task {}` work is never cancelled. During `work`, only "Use this" and "Preview" are disabled; Retake, the chips, the marks, Clear marks and the navigation Back button stay live. A cut can take up to 270 s.
     - Retake during "Use this": `finish` later pushes `.designReady` on top of the camera screen.
     - Back during a redo "Use this": `isRedo` is re-evaluated against the changed path, and `createPath.removeLast()` pops Design ready (or another screen) instead.
   - **Fix:**
     - Disable Retake, the chips, the marks and the mode picker while `work != nil`, and use `.navigationBarBackButtonHidden(work != nil)`.
     - Keep the `Task` handle and cancel it in `onDisappear`.
     - Capture `isRedo` when the work starts, and only navigate if `.adjustCutout` is still the top of `createPath`.

### Minor

1. **The letterbox unit test covers a function the screen never calls.**
   - **Where:** `ios/App/Services/CutoutGeometry.swift:8`, `ios/Tests/CutoutTests.swift:22-29`.
   - **What:** `fitRect` is the only letterbox math, but the view instead forces an `imageSize`-aspect frame (`AdjustCutoutView.swift:166-171`) around an inner `scaledToFit` image. The real view-to-image mapping (frame `(0, 0, geo.size)`) is never tested with an offset. If the local photo's aspect ever differs from `imageSize`, the marks drift silently.
   - **Fix:** either compute the frame with `fitRect` inside the view, or assert that the aspect ratios match and fetch the original when they do not. Test the composition the view actually uses.

2. **A zero `imageSize` breaks the layout.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:167`, `:314`.
   - **What:** the backend can answer `imageSize {w: 0, h: 0}` (`meta.width ?? 0`), which makes `aspectRatio(size.w / size.h)` NaN or ∞.
   - **Fix:** treat a size with w or h ≤ 0 as nil.

3. **The `select` path loses product notices and cuts twice.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:457`, `:466-467`; `CutoutGeometry.swift:72`.
   - **What:**
     - `select` hides `details`, although on the server `selectCandidate` is `cleanupArtwork` and also rebuilds products.
     - A Preview with exactly one id is a cleanup. "Use this" then sends `select`, so the preview is not reused and the cut is made a second time.
   - **Fix:** send `cleanup {candidateIds: [id]}` whenever products may exist (redo mode). Compare previews by the composed request rather than the action.

4. **In redo mode, "Use this" with nothing changed still re-cuts.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:455-476`.
   - **What:** it calls `select` again, which costs time and rebuilds products.
   - **Fix:** when nothing has changed, just pop back to Design ready.

5. **Some cancellations show up as errors.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:430`, `:448`, `:471`.
   - **What:** `catch is CancellationError` misses `URLError.cancelled`.
   - **Fix:** use the existing `APIError.isCancellation`.

6. **The checkerboard is invisible with Increase Contrast.**
   - **Where:** `ios/App/Components/Checkerboard.swift:9`, `:18`, with `ThemeColors.swift:50`.
   - **What:** in high contrast, `surface` and `surfaceAlt` are both `HighContrast.surface` (white), so the board is flat white.
   - **Fix:** use `border` at low opacity, or a token pair that differs.

7. **VoiceOver speech is doubled, and the hints break Apple's guidance.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:395`, `:498-499`.
   - **What:**
     - Toggling posts a "Selected" / "Not selected" announcement on top of VoiceOver's own trait and value change, so it is spoken twice.
     - `Text("")` as the selected value adds an empty catalog key.
     - The hints begin "Double-tap to…", against Apple's hint guidance.
   - **Fix:** drop the announcement, set no value when selected, and write hints as "Leaves it out of the cutout".

8. **Some spots on the photo cannot take a mark.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:185-194`.
   - **What:** each mark and chip is a 48 pt button above the gesture layer. No mark can be added within 24 pt of an existing mark or under a chip, and marks near the edge are partly clipped.
   - **Fix:** mention this in the instruction, or put the chips outside the piece.

9. **A failed load in redo mode can leave a blank photo with no error.**
   - **Where:** `AdjustCutoutView.swift:423-434`.
   - **What:** when there is no local photo but `imageSize` is known, a failed `GET /artworks/{id}` shows an empty photo area with no error (the error is only set when `imageSize` is nil).
   - **Fix:** show the error whenever `photo == nil && originalURL == nil`.

10. **The mock does not hold the app to the contract, and several paths are untested.**
    - **Where:** `ios/scripts/mock_api.py:104-110`.
    - **What:** the mock accepts any cleanup body. It does not apply the limits (1–6 ids, 1–10 points), the label enum, the box ordering or the negative-only rule, and it never answers 422 or non-empty `details`. The UI tests do not cover a box, Remove area, 422, the product notices, or Retake clearing marks.
    - **Fix:** port the `CleanupBody` rules into the mock (answer 400 like the server). Add a code prefix that makes the mock answer 422 and non-empty `details`, and one UI test for each.
