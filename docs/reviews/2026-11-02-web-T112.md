### Minor
1. **Tapping the sheet's padding closes it.** `web/components/product/CheckoutSheet.tsx:23`
   - What: `onClick={(e) => { if (e.target === e.currentTarget) e.currentTarget.close(); }}` is meant to close on a backdrop click. But the `<dialog>` itself has padding (`Product.module.css:255`, `--space-xxl` top and the 24 px gutters), so a click on that padding also has the dialog as its target.
   - Verified: on the 390 sheet, a click 8 px in from the left edge closed it.
   - Failure scenario: a buyer with a tremor, or someone tapping beside a field on a phone, loses the sheet in the middle of filling it in. The typed values survive because the inputs stay mounted, but they have to reopen the sheet.
   - Fix: move the padding onto an inner wrapper and set the dialog's padding to 0. Or close only when the click point is outside `getBoundingClientRect()`.
2. **"Draws by hand" is shown for every maker.** `web/components/shop/MeetArtist.tsx:55` (`shop.makerMethod`) and `web/components/product/ProductView.tsx:40` (`product.makerMethod`).
   - What: every maker card and every product artist row says "Draws by hand" / "draws by hand". No creator field backs this.
   - Why it matters: the board itself gives different makers different methods (Priya: "Paints by hand"), and the PM default in `docs/design/copy-v3-web.md` (Meet the makers and the "Strings with no board" list) is to omit the chip until a field exists. The report cites an earlier ruling accepting it as sample data, so the two PM statements conflict.
   - Failure scenario: a real maker who paints or makes collage is described with a method that isn't theirs, on a site whose pitch is that the art is the artist's own.
   - Fix (recommended): follow the later copy doc and drop both strings and the chip. Or the PM confirms the earlier ruling in writing.
3. **Dead `voice` exclusion in FilterTabs.** `web/components/site/FilterTabs.tsx:8` and `:18`
   - What: `Exclude<ShopFilter, "voice">` remains, but T111's `ShopFilter` no longer has `voice`, so the exclusion is a no-op.
   - Why it matters: it is a leftover reference to the removed filter, and it confuses the next grep for `voice`.
   - Fix: use `ShopFilter`.
4. **Board status rows not updated.** `docs/design/screens.md:61` and `:66`
   - What: the `2018:1789` and `2019:2004` rows (and the 390 / empty rows 62, 63, 67) still say `pending (v3, T110)`, although this branch builds them.
   - Fix: mark them built (T112) here, or hand the update explicitly to T115.
5. **Font comment differs slightly from the handoff.** `web/app/layout.tsx:7-8`
   - What: the comment says "Design v3 (T110)" and adds a parenthetical. `docs/handoffs/design.md:23` asks for "Sky v3 (T110)" exactly. The code lines match the handoff.
   - Fix: optional, comment only.
