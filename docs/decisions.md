## 2026-10-29 · Studio-issued creator codes; hidden creators for testing (T070)
- **Decision:** no self-sign-up this week. The studio adds a creator on `/admin` and hands over a code (shown once, with a QR of `/create/code?c=<CODE>` and the iOS link `makerstory://code/<CODE>`). ## 2026-10-29 · Photos of objects: two SAM phrases, mask finishing, soft alpha (T080)
- **Decision:** a photo or canvas upload is searched with `SAM_PHRASES = ["drawing", "handmade object"]` in parallel (two SAM calls), results are merged (IoU > 0.8 = same thing, higher score kept), anything covering > 95 % of the frame is dropped (paper, table), scores under 0.1 are dropped when anything scores higher, at most 6 candidates. Every cut goes through `finishMask` (fill holes, close ≈ 2 px at request scale, drop specks < 0.5 % of the main blob except pieces the creator combined, linear resize to a soft 0–255 alpha). The opaque original is upscaled before the cut when the cut would be under 900 px on its short side; the RGBA cut is never resampled. `removeBackground` takes the 8-bit alpha.
- **Why:** play-test cut-outs of a toy and a wallet had white holes and half-erased backgrounds: SAM's hard 0/1 mask was used as alpha with no hole filling, and the phrase `"drawing"` splits or misses 3D things. The sticker print hid the holes (white under a filled outline); totes and the "My designs" tiles did not.
- **Phrase choice:** the plan named `"object"`; the live check found `"object"` returns nothing on a knitted bear on wood while `"handmade object"` finds it and also finds a wallet and clay figure (docs/architecture.md, T080 live findings). It is one constant in `lib/ai/meta/sam.ts`.
- **Alternatives:** one phrase per request with a client-chosen word (rejected: the creator should not have to name the thing); a third phrase (`"toy"`): rejected for now (3× cost), revisit if candidates miss things.

## 2026-10-29 · Adjust API: combine candidates, labelled points, a box (T080)
- **Decision:** `POST /artworks/{id}/cleanup { candidateIds?, points?: {x,y,label}[], box? }`. `candidateIds` = union of stored candidate masks (no model call); `points`/`box` = one SAM object (`buildObjectPrompt`, labels passed through), unioned with the candidates. Negative points do not trim the candidate masks (documented; the client sends a box + points without `candidateIds` to trim). Coordinates are pixels of the stored original; `imageSize` is returned by `POST /artworks` (photo) and the new `GET /artworks/{id}`. `select` is now `cleanup` with one candidate. The old `phrase` field is gone (no client used it).
- **After a product exists:** cleanup rebuilds every live product's print file(s) and mockup (`rebuildProductFiles`) in the same request; ids, status, price and listing copy stay. The listing's stored print check is not recomputed (the art is the same subject; approve re-checks only when the agent runs again).
- **Delete:** `DELETE /artworks/{id}` soft-deletes the artwork and sets its products to `archived` in one batch. The shop (`listPublishedProducts`, `getPublicProduct`) shows only `published` products of live artworks; orders and `/t/{token}` do not filter by product status or artwork deletion, so they keep rendering.
- **`imageSize`** is stored in `artworks.shaping_trace.imageSize` at upload (no schema change); older rows read it from the stored original.

## 2026-10-29 · T080 fix round 1: adjust semantics, safe product rebuild, speck rule, container rule
- **Negative points apply to the combined selection:** a negative point removes the connected piece of a chosen candidate it lands on (no model call); candidateIds + negative-only points are allowed. A SAM-prompted subtraction (segment the shadow under a negative point and subtract it) was **not** built: it costs a SAM call per negative point and SAM's object for a tapped shadow often includes the object it is attached to; the creator can instead send a box around what should stay. · Review important 1.
- **Product rebuild on re-cut is per product and parallel** (`Promise.allSettled`); failures are returned in `details.failedProductIds` and do not touch `lastError` (the cut succeeded). The print check is recomputed into `listing.printCheck`; a published product whose check now fails goes back to draft (logged). · Review important 2.
- **Speck rule:** a piece is dropped only when under 0.5 % of its part's main blob **and** under max(0.02 % of the frame, 64 px). Dots, stars and short strokes of a drawing stay. · Review important 3.
- **Paper/table guard:** a candidate covering > 40 % of the frame whose box contains another candidate's box is dropped; a single candidate covering > 50 % is not auto-selected (the picker shows it). Checked live on three drawing-on-paper photos (docs/architecture.md). · Review important 4.
- **Archived products** reject `run`/`approve` with `409 product_archived`. · Review minor 8.
