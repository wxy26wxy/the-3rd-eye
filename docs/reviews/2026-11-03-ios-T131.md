### Minor
1. **Re-marking the piece after a change keeps the change on screen** (`ios/App/Screens/Create/YourCutoutView.swift:427` vs `MakingCutoutView.swift:54`).
   - An area cut calls `flow.resetChange()`, but "Apply cutout" on the piece's Your cutout does not; it only updates the chain's base.
   - Failure scenario: change the art, go Back twice to the piece's Your cutout, fix the edges with marks, then Use this. Change it still shows the old change (built on the old cut), and "Looks good as is" makes the product from that change rather than from the freshly marked piece. It is visible on screen ("Your art"), but surprising.
   - The UI test asserts this behaviour ("Change it still shows the change on screen").
   - Fix: on a re-cut of the piece, set `changedArtworkId = nil` (keep the chain for Change it again), or `resetChange()` as the area cut does.
2. **Remove area has no VoiceOver path, but its hint promises one** (`YourCutoutView.swift:205-206`, `:255-258`).
   - The mode hint says "With VoiceOver, use the actions on the photo", but the photo offers only "Add the area around your art".
   - Fix: say only what exists (for example "Then tap on the photo"), or add a "Leave out the area around …" action.
   - Accepted otherwise: the brief keeps only the whole-art actions.
3. **"Back to my original" now means one step back** (`Screens/Create/BeforeAfterView.swift:42`).
   - With two or more changes the label is no longer literally true. The announcement does say "Back to your last change".
   - Fix: a copy or designer decision (for example "Back one change" when the base is a change). Route it to the copy lane with the 8 new lines.
4. **Three pinned buttons on Your cutout** (Use this, Apply cutout, Start over).
   - At AX3 the bar takes about a third of the screen. The content scrolls, and every primary stays reachable, so this is acceptable for the freeze.
   - Later: move Start over into the content as a text action, or show Apply cutout only once there are marks.
5. **`ios/build/` (183 MB of DerivedData) is back in the worktree.** It is ignored; delete it on a disk-tight Mac.
6. **Left by the implementer, agreed as minor:**
   - Story video Skip does not send `dismissStoryDraft` (consistency item 9). Do it in a follow-up, or record it in the T132 docs.
   - docs/api.md "Creator flow (round 2b)" step 5 puts "Looks good as is" on Your cutout; it is on Change it. This is T132's.
