## Checks
- **Maths.**

  - `clipToBox` copies each row inside the box, an exact AND with the box.
  - In `finishArtwork`, `clipAlpha` clamps `box × k` at the cut size. Since `floor(x0)·k ≤ floor(x0·k)` and `ceil(x1)·k ≥ ceil(x1·k)`, the alpha clip is never looser than the request-scale clip, so nothing spills past the frame.
