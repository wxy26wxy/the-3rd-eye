Reviewer: independent; read-only on the checkout. Probes ran from a copy of `ios/` in the scratchpad; nothing was added to the branch.

## Strengths
- The component layer matches the brief. Every `TextRole` resolves to `Tokens.FontName` ("Inter Variable", registered in `UIAppFonts`), and Dynamic Type still scales through `relativeTo:`. `DisplayText` has no stroke and no shadow, and its tilt comes from `Tokens.displayTilt`, which is 0. `PillButton` is 56/50 pt (`Size.cta` / `ctaSecondary`) with a 48 pt arrow circle, and disabled uses `Opacity.disabled`. `Card`/`ChoiceCard` have a 28 radius, a 1.5 pt `Border.card`, and selected = accent + `accentBorder` + `RadioMark` with a check. `GrainOverlay`, `ScallopDivider`, `Starburst` and `hardShadow` are no-ops. `StepPill` is a 1 × 1 accessibility-only element. `TagMascot` poses map to `mascot-book` / `mascot-front` / `mascot-magnifier.mp4`, and the optional `label` keeps the old accessibility behaviour.
- `LoopingVideo` is muted, loops with `AVPlayerLooper`, and drops its looper on dismantle. I confirmed Reduce Motion on the simulator: Finding shows the still `mascot-front` on `sky`.
- The tab bar meets three of the four ruling conditions, verified by running it (see Test runs):
  - XCUITest finds exactly one `TabBar` with four buttons, and the selected state follows each tap.
  - Re-tapping the selected tab pops to the root.
  - The bar is removed while the keyboard is up.
  - The bar is stacked below the content, not drawn over it.
- No raw colours outside `Theme/` (grep = 0). `pnpm tokens` leaves no diff. `check_clean.sh` is ok. No Swift warnings in a clean build (only the AppIntents notice). `MARKETING_VERSION` "0.5.0" / `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` "1". `"%lld new"` is in `Localizable.xcstrings`. UI tests are untouched (no diff under `ios/UITests`). All 6 commits are by Xinyao Wang <wxy26wxy@gmail.com>, with no attribution lines and no assistant mentions in the diff.
- The docs are present: the handoff "Sky components (T103)" section with asset steps, the progress T103 section, and the decisions entry.

## Issues

### Critical

### Important

1. **Tall FlowScreens leave the primary action below the fold; there is no pinned bar.** (`ios/App/Components/FlowScreen.swift:20-51`; same pattern in `ios/App/Components/LoadingScreen.swift:26-34` (error actions) and `ios/App/Screens/Onboarding/WelcomeView.swift:31-35`)
   - What: `actions` sit inside the `ScrollView`, after `content` and a `Spacer`.
   - Why: the ruling in force says tall FlowScreens must pin the primary action in a bottom bar while the content scrolls. FlowScreen is a T103 component.
   - Failure scenario, measured:
     - Standard size: Adjust cutout, Design ready and You're live put Use this, Yes list it! and Make another below the fold. The implementer's own screenshots show this (`t103-shots/05-adjust`, `07-ready`, `08-live`).
     - Accessibility size 3: Pick "Next" is at y = 902, with the tab bar top at 768. Adjust "Use this" is at y = 1679. Design ready "Yes, list it!" is at y = 1038. Welcome "Start creating" is at y = 897 in an 874 pt window. None is visible without scrolling.
     - A VoiceOver or Switch Control user has to find the action at the end of the content. A sighted user on Design ready sees a blank area and no button.
   - Fix:
     - Move `actions` out of the scroll view into `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)`: a white bar with a 1 pt top `border`, page padding, and `Space.s` between buttons. Content stays scrollable above it.
     - Do the same for the LoadingScreen error actions and Welcome's "Start creating".
     - Since the bar rides above the keyboard, check Listing details and Code entry with the keyboard up.
     - Update the decision entry and the handoff line that still call the pinned bar "an open question".

2. **Increase Contrast makes Welcome less legible: white text on the photo turns ink.** (`ios/App/Theme/ThemeColors.swift:55` (`textOnDark: Tokens.HighContrast.text`), used by `ios/App/Screens/Onboarding/WelcomeView.swift:12,16` via `DisplayText(onPhoto:)` and by `ios/App/Theme/LogoMark.swift:14`)
   - What: in the high-contrast palette `textOnDark` is ink (#111827). T103 now puts `textOnDark` on the sky photo behind a 35 % black scrim.
   - Failure scenario: with Increase Contrast on (screenshot from my probe), the wordmark, the subtitle "Turn your art into things people buy." and the logo mark render ink on a dark blue-grey panel. By my estimate that is about 3:1, under the 4.5:1 needed for body text. Without Increase Contrast the same text is white, at roughly 5–6:1. The setting meant to raise contrast lowers it on the first screen.
   - Fix: keep `textOnDark` white in the high-contrast palette (`Tokens.HighContrast.primaryText` or `Semantic.textOnDark`). For high contrast, also raise the scrim opacity, or put the Welcome text on an ink panel. Add a `SystemAccessibilityTests` case asserting `highContrast.textOnDark` is light.

3. **Tab bar: VoiceOver's "tab, n of 4" is claimed but not shown; the exposed structure makes it doubtful.** (`ios/App/Components/HomeTabBar.swift:85-92,161-164`; `docs/decisions.md` T103 entry)
   - What XCUITest exposes: `TabBar 'Tab Bar'` → `Other` → `Other` → 4 × `Button` ('Create' Selected, 'My designs', 'Orders', 'Messages'). The value is empty with no badge.
   - Each Button still lists child `Image` elements with SF Symbol default labels ('Add', 'Grid View', 'Box', 'Comment Left') and `StaticText` children, despite `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)`.
   - In a native bar the buttons are direct children of the TabBar element. Here two anonymous containers sit between them, and the items are SwiftUI buttons without a tab trait.
   - The ruling accepts the hosted bar only if VoiceOver reads "tab, n of 4" with the selected state. Nobody has checked this, neither the implementer nor me: no device, and XCUITest does not expose spoken output. Yet `docs/decisions.md` states it as fact.
   - Failure scenario: VoiceOver reads "Create, selected, button" with no "tab" and no position, and the SF Symbol names may be reachable as extra stops.
   - Fix:
     - Check it once with VoiceOver on, on a device or the simulator with Accessibility Inspector.
     - If the reading is wrong, flatten the tree: `HostTabBar.accessibilityElements` returns four `UIAccessibilityElement`s, one per tab. Each has the tab's label, `.button` (+ `.selected`) traits, the badge value, a frame from the item, and an activation that calls `choose`. The bar keeps its `.tabBar` trait.
     - Also `.accessibilityHidden(true)` the item `Image`s.
     - Until it is verified, change the decision text from "VoiceOver gets…" to what was measured.

### Minor

4. `ios/App/Theme/Starburst.swift:3-4`: the doc comment says the text "is still read by VoiceOver"; the body is `EmptyView`, so nothing is read. Related: `MethodRow` drops its `eyebrow` from the accessibility label (`ios/App/Theme/MethodRow.swift:53`), so "NO PEN NEEDED!" is no longer spoken on Choose. Fix the comment, and decide whether the eyebrow belongs in the label (T104 copy may remove it anyway).
5. `ios/App/Components/HomeTabBar.swift:85-92`: the tabs are `onTapGesture` views, not `Button`s. They have no pressed state and no Large Content Viewer. The system bar shows the long-press HUD at accessibility sizes, and labels here stay small (`minimumScaleFactor 0.7`). Add `.accessibilityShowsLargeContentViewer { … }` to each item.
6. `ios/App/Components/LoadingScreen.swift:88-99` (`SkyCard`): with Reduce Motion, `mascot-front` (2x = 204 × 240, an 8-bit colormap PNG, no 3x) is stretched to the full ~342 pt card width on a 3x screen. It looks visibly posterised and blurred (probe screenshot). Ask design for a larger `mascot-front`, or size the still mascot to the asset rather than to the card.
7. `ios/App/Components/HomeTabBar.swift:142-151`: the host hides every non-hosted `UITabBar` subview on each layout pass, which depends on UIKit internals (the implementer flagged this). It is acceptable for the demo; keep a UI test that asserts `app.tabBars.count == 1` and the four buttons, so an iOS update that breaks it fails loudly.

### Deviations
- **Bar stacked under the content instead of `safeAreaInset`: acceptable.** The ruling asks for the primary action pinned while the content scrolls, and the stacked bar does exactly that. Content ending at the bar also means nothing scrolls under it, and nothing can be tapped through it. The reason given (5 Adjust UI tests tapped through the inset) is plausible and matches the earlier tab-bar finding. No fix needed.
- **Design detail keeps its five actions after the content (`pinsActions: false`): acceptable for v0.7-rc1, not for v0.7.**
  - Measured at AX3, "See it in the shop" is at y 905 and "Edit listing" at y 971, below the fold behind the preview.
  - Pinning all five buttons would leave no room, so the call is reasonable for now. But the ruling's intent (the primary action reachable without scrolling) is unmet on this screen.
  - Design detail is a per-screen layout and its board is a T104 item. T104 should pin the screen's primary action ("See it in the shop", or "Edit listing" for a draft) with `.actionBar` and leave the rest in the content.
  - Logged as Minor R1 below.
- **Adjust pins only Use this: acceptable.** Use this is the primary action. Apply cutout and Retake are secondary tools and now follow the tools in the content (Retake at y 1713 at AX3). At AX3 the photo area above the bar is short (about 220 pt visible) but it scrolls. No fix needed.
- **Minor 5 deferred (Large Content Viewer removed because the app stopped reporting idle): acceptable for v0.7-rc1.**
  - Tab labels stay one line at accessibility sizes, which they do legibly (the 13 pt label, minimum scale 0.7).
  - The idle stall would have made the UI suite unusable, so dropping the SwiftUI modifier is the right trade.
  - A follow-up that avoids SwiftUI: add a `UILargeContentViewerInteraction` to `HostTabBar`. Its delegate returns the tab's title and symbol image for the touched slot, and it works with the `TabElement`s already there.
  - Logged as Minor R2.
