## Important
1. **Auto-speech on arrival is likely cancelled by the previous screen.** `FlowScreen`/`LoadingScreen`/`TabScreen` stop speech on disappear; on iOS 17 NavigationStack the destination's `onAppear` usually precedes the source's `onDisappear`, so the voice-result description and spoken confirmations are silenced for non-VoiceOver users. Fix: stop speech on route change, not on disappear. → fix round 1
2. **Voice "yes" approval skips the title/price validation** that the tap path enforces (`ListingDetailsView`). Could list at $0.00. Fix: validate inside `approve()`. → fix round 1
3. **Orders persist across sign-out** (`OrdersStore` never reset), leaking one creator's orders to the next on a shared device. Fix: `OrdersStore.reset()` from `signOut()`. → fix round 1
