## Issues

### Critical

### Important
1. **Tall stickers are cropped on shop cards, gallery slide 1 and its thumbnails, and the phone preview.** This breaks R1's "never cropped".
   - Where: `web/components/site/ProductCard.module.css:28-35` (`.cutout`), `web/components/product/Product.module.css:49` (`.slideContain`), `web/components/shop/Shop.module.css:221` (`.phoneThumb img`).
   - What: each `<img>` is `width/height: 100%` inside a **grid** container with `overflow: hidden`. As a grid item, a replaced element keeps `min-height: auto`, so its automatic minimum size is its aspect-derived height. A preview taller than the box makes the `<img>` taller than its container.
   - Measured at 390:
     - Shop tile: a 846×1200 preview → img 163×232 in a 163×173 picture; three of eight tiles on the home page are cut at the bottom.
     - Product page "Shocked Pink Sea Star": slide 1 img 342×451 in a 342×312 slide. The sticker runs off the bottom and the 12 % padding is lost.
     - Thumbnail: img 74×100 in 74×59, and it overflows (`overflow: visible`).
   - Failure scenario: a judge opens the shop or a product page and sees portrait stickers cut off at the bottom, which is exactly what item 7 asked to stop. The existing test only asserts the `contain` class, so it cannot catch this.
   - Fix: add `min-height: 0; min-width: 0;` to `.cutout`, `.slideContain` and `.phoneThumb img`, or position the image absolutely (`inset: 0`) inside the picture box, as `.slideCover` already does. Confirmed in the page: setting `min-height: 0` on one tile image brought it from 232 to 173 px (fits). Re-check at 390 and 1440 with a portrait preview.

### Minor
2. **Small frames are hard to move with a pointer.** `web/components/create/area.ts:13` (`AREA_MIN` 5 %) with 44 px handles in `Create.module.css:297-311`.
   - At the minimum (seen: 4 % × 54 % after Shift+Left), the edge and corner handles cover the whole frame. There is no inside left to drag, and the two side handles overlap.
   - The keyboard still works and pointer users can grow it from an edge. Optional fix: raise the minimum so the frame stays at least about 3 × 44 px on screen, or keep `move` reachable by drawing handles outside the frame line.
3. **Dead cut-out helpers kept alive only by tests.** `web/components/create/cutout.ts`: `makeRequest`, `boxAround`, `centrePoint`, `isStuck`, `leavesNothing`, `wholeImageBox`, `chooseAction`, `sameRequest`, `needsTrimAdvice`, `AppliedCutout`, `initialChoice`. Their only users are `cutout.ts` and `test/cutout.test.ts`. Optional fix: delete them and their tests in a follow-up.
4. **The redo return route has no test.** `StoryVideo.tsx` `done = redo ? "/create/product" : "/create/live"`. Row and view tests exist, but nothing asserts that a redo save or the story-draft path returns to `/create/product`. Optional fix: move `done` into `flow.ts` (`storyVideoDone(redo)`) with a one-line test.
5. **The story row's `failed` flag is sticky.** `StoryVideoRow.tsx:74-77`: closing and reopening Watch keeps "We couldn't load your video" until the page remounts (the route stays mounted under `cacheComponents`), also after Record again. Optional fix: reset `failed` in `onWatch`.
6. **Leftover props.** `ChoosePattern.tsx` passes `busy={false}` / `disabled={false}`, and `ChoosePatternView` still has the busy footer and the error notice, which are now unreachable. Cosmetic.
