### Important
1. **Design detail · Didn't work has no primary action** — `ios/App/Screens/Home/DesignDetailView.swift:124-126` and `:168`.
   - What: `primary(_:)` returns `nil` when `isReadyForProducts` is false and nothing is live, so a failed, needs-a-cutout or cutting-out design has no pinned bar and "Re-do the cutout" is drawn as a secondary pill.
   - Why it matters: board `2016:1284` (B2 row "Design detail · Didn't work", "same pattern serves Needs a cutout / Cutting out…") draws "Re-do the cutout" as the black primary with Delete under it. This is the one recovery action on that screen and the brief's rule is "pin the primary action".
   - Failure scenario: a creator opens a design marked Didn't work; the screen shows two equal-weight outlined pills (Re-do the cutout, Delete in red) and nothing pinned. At accessibility sizes, both sit below the picture, badge and explanation, so the recovery action can be off screen.
   - Fix: add a `case redo` to `Primary`; return it when the artwork is not ready and has no published product; render the existing Re-do button with `kind: .primary` in the bar and skip it in `secondaryActions`. Keep `testFailedDesignOffersRedoButNotProducts` and add an assertion that "Re-do the cutout" is hittable without scrolling at the largest text size (or check it in the AX3 pass).

1. **Arithmetic on tokens produces untokenised sizes in screens** — `ios/App/Screens/Create/AdjustCutoutView.swift:332, 388, 391, 435, 437, 483, 494, 737`; `ios/App/Screens/Home/MyDesignsView.swift:84`; `ios/App/Screens/Orders/OrdersView.swift:49-50`; `ios/App/Screens/Onboarding/WelcomeView.swift:73`.

### New findings
- **Minor N1:** at accessibility sizes the unread count in an order row spills out of its circle. File: `ios/App/Screens/Orders/OrdersView.swift:75`.
  - What: `.frame(minWidth: badgeSize, minHeight: badgeSize).background(Circle())`. The caption digit grows taller than it is wide, and a `Circle` in a tall frame takes the smaller side.
  - Evidence: the AX3 shot `22-orders.png` shows the white "2" running past the ink circle's top and bottom onto the white card, so part of the digit is invisible.
  - Impact: VoiceOver still reads "2 new messages", but sighted low-vision users get a clipped number.
  - Fix: make the frame square (`.aspectRatio(1, contentMode: .fill)` on the frame, or a `Capsule` with equal padding).
  - Introduced: the frame code is from T103. T104 changed the fill colour from danger to ink, which is when the clipped part became white-on-white.
