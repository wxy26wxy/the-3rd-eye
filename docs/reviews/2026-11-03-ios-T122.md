## Issues

### Critical
None.

### Important
1. **Handles are well under 44 pt when the frame is at the photo's edge** (`ios/App/Screens/Create/AdjustAreaView.swift:116-131`, `:123`).
   - What: `AreaDragSurface` fills only the photo overlay. A corner or edge on the photo's border gets only the half of its 44 pt zone that lies inside the photo. A corner gets one 22×22 pt quadrant.
   - Why it matters: this is the starting frame whenever no drawing was found (`AreaFrame.whole`), and often after Grow or after a bbox clamped at an edge. R2 asks for 44 pt handles.
   - Failure scenario: a creator with limited fine motor control, on a photo with no detected drawing, has to hit a 22 pt corner on the photo's edge to shrink the frame. The green 20 pt card inset around it does not respond.
   - Fix: give the drag surface (and the hit test in `AreaFrame.handle`) `minTarget/2` of outset beyond the photo, then convert points back by that offset. Or inset the drawn frame by half a target when it sits on the edge. Add a UI test that drags the whole-photo corner of the `NONE…` mock.

### Minor
1. **A whole-photo frame swallows vertical scrolling on the photo** (`AdjustAreaView.swift` `AreaDragSurface.gestureRecognizerShouldBegin`).
   - Any touch inside the frame starts a "move". At the whole-photo size that move is a no-op, but the page does not scroll from there.
   - At AX3 the photo fills most of the screen; the page still scrolls from the title area.
   - Fix: do not begin a `.move` pan when the frame already covers the whole photo, or begin it only after a short press.
2. **Tote picture on Listing details sits on the grey card, not the tote-colour art card** (`ListingDetailsView.swift:100`). R1 says "cleaned art on the tote-colour card (existing `StickerCard`/art card)". `StickerCard` was deleted in this branch. The art is shown at its own proportions, so the item-5 concern is met; only the colour treatment differs.
3. **My story video: VoiceOver hears the replace note twice, and the disability card promises an edit that is not here** (`Screens/Listing/StoryVideoLibraryView.swift:213-219`, `:253-254`).
   - "Record again" has the replace note as its hint, and the same sentence sits right under the button as text.
   - The read-only disability card says "You can change this later." There is no control on this screen, and the setting can only change by recording again.
   - Fix: a shorter hint (for example "Opens the camera"), and either a pointer such as "Change it when you record again" or the toggle itself.
4. **`ios/build/` (173 MB of DerivedData) is back in the worktree.** It is ignored, but disk on this Mac is tight. Delete it when convenient (not done here: read-only review).
5. **`docs/progress.md` has no T122 entry** (concern 5 in the report). Add one in the fix round.
